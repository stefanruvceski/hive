# ADR 0004 — Businesses are shared: one core table for Money and Wallet

- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decided by:** the maintainer (review of the Wallet/Money PR); details marked "agent" were decided while building
- **Amends:** [ADR 0002](0002-money-spaces-and-rates.md) §1 (where the business list lives)

## Context

ADR 0002 kept the businesses in `money_businesses`. Wallet receipts also belong to a business (the buyer's PIB), and the same business was typed twice, once in each module, or Wallet offered to save one Money already had. Modules never import each other ([ADR 0001](0001-modules-sqlite-native-only.md)), so the list has to live below both.

## Decision

1. **One core table, `core_businesses`** (`id`, `name`, `pib` unique, `archived_at`, `created_at`), in `src/lib/db/schema.ts`. Money's tables point their foreign keys at it.
2. **A shared hook**, `src/hooks/use-businesses.ts`: `useBusinesses`, `useSaveBusiness`, `useEnsureBusiness`, `useArchiveBusiness`, the pure `businessByPib` and `ensureBusiness(tx, pib, name)` for consumers that write in a transaction (Money's receipt import). PIB helpers are in `src/utils/pib.ts`.
3. **Data-preserving migration.** `money_businesses` is renamed to `core_businesses` (generated by drizzle-kit as a rename), so every row and id survives and Money entries keep their business.
4. **A PIB is one business.** A receipt with a buyer PIB finds the business by PIB and shows its name; nobody is asked to save it again. Only a PIB that no module knows becomes a new business (named `Business <PIB>` until renamed).
5. **Wallet's owner is a choice** among the known businesses (or Personal), with typing a new PIB only as a fallback.
6. **The shop is not a business.** The shop's PIB on a receipt (who sold) is kept apart from the buyer's PIB (who owns the receipt). Shops get a name per PIB in Wallet's own table, not in `core_businesses`.

## Consequences

- One migration. Money's `business-queries.ts` re-exports the shared hook so its screens and tests keep their import.
- Money no longer owns the business rows: deleting Money would not delete the businesses Wallet uses.
